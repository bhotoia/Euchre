How to play

Deck — 24 cards: 9, 10, J, Q, K, A in four suits.

Teams — You + Partner (Us) vs West + East (Them).

Trump — Each hand one suit is trump. The right bower (Jack of trump) is highest; the left bower (other Jack of the same color) is second and counts as trump.

Bidding — A card is turned up. In turn, order it up (makes its suit trump) or pass. If all pass, name any other suit or pass again.

Going alone — The maker may play without their partner for bonus points.

Play — Follow the led suit if you can. Highest trump wins, else highest card of the led suit.

Scoring — Makers: 3–4 tricks = 1, all 5 = 2 (4 alone). Euchred (makers fail) = 2 to defenders. First to 10 wins.

Trump order (highest → lowest), with ♥ as trump:

J♥right J♦left A♥ K♥ Q♥ 10♥ 9♥

Both Jacks of the trump colour are the two strongest cards. Everything else ranks A–9 in its own suit.
