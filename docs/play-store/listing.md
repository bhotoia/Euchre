## Full description

Play a polished offline Euchre game built for quick phone sessions and deeper practice.

Euchre includes Solo play, Train mode, Daily Deals, and tournament progression. Learn the game with an in-game coach that grades bids and card plays, explains better options, and tracks your improvement over time.

Features:

- Standard 24-card Euchre rules
- Solo games against Easy, Normal, or Hard AI opponents
- Train mode with move-by-move coaching and practical explanations
- Offline Daily Deal with the same date-seeded challenge for everyone
- Tournament ladder with unlockable card packs, badges, titles, rivals, and allies
- Persistent local profile, stats, trophies, cosmetics, saves, and preferences
- English and Mandarin interface support
- Slow and Fast game-speed settings
- Offline play with no account required

Privacy-friendly by design:

- No sign-in
- No ads
- No analytics
- No crash reporting SDK
- No telemetry
- No cloud sync
- No backend service
- Game progress and settings stay on the device

Euchre is a trick-taking card game for players who want a focused, offline table: play a hand, sharpen your bidding, chase tournament rewards, or use the coach to understand why one card beats another.
