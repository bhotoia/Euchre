# Euchre Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 30, 2026

Euchre is an offline card game. This version does not use accounts, cloud sync, ads, analytics, crash reporting, telemetry, online multiplayer, or a backend service.

## Information Stored On Your Device

The app stores gameplay and preference data locally on your device so the game can remember your progress. This may include:

- Player and team names entered in the app
- Saved games
- Settings such as sound, haptics, language, game speed, difficulty, and coaching preferences
- Career stats, badges, ranks, trophies, titles, card packs, allies, rivals, tournament progress, prestige progress, and Daily Deal history

This data is stored only in the app's local storage on your device. It is not sent to the developer by this version of the app.

## Network, Accounts, And Third Parties

This version does not require sign-in and does not transmit personal information to a server. It does not include advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, crash reporting SDKs, or social features.

If a future version adds optional Google Play Games, cloud saves, analytics, crash reporting, ads, or account features, this policy and the app's store disclosures will be updated before those features are released.

## Data Deletion

You can delete local app data inside the app by going to Settings, then using Reset stats or Reset everything. You can also remove app data through Android system settings by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

## Children's Privacy

This app is a card game and is not designed to collect personal information from children. This version does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.
